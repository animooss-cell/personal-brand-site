# Design references

These files are reference material only. Nothing here has been applied to the site. Before any UI change, read `audit.md` and the chosen reference below, and follow the "Design improvement rules" in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

- `audit.md`: a review of the current site (home, services, about, contact) at 390, 768 and 1440px, with problems listed by priority. Screenshots and the raw inspector output are in `screenshots/<page>/`.
- `references/`: three DESIGN.md files copied unchanged from [VoltAgent/awesome-design-md](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md) (commit `3b60532`, MIT licence, see `references/LICENSE-awesome-design-md.txt`). They describe other companies' design systems. Use them for ideas about structure, proportion and component behaviour, never as a source of their logos, fonts or colours.

Ground rules when reading any of them: aiabed.ir keeps its own brand (green `#00C389`, dark blue `#0F4C81`, Vazirmatn) and its Persian, right-to-left layout. Their Latin typefaces map to Vazirmatn weights, and negative letter-spacing is never applied to Persian text, because it breaks the joined letters. Where a reference contradicts the house rules of the design-taste-frontend skill (gradients, resting shadows, hairline boxes, eyebrow labels), the skill's rules win.

## The three styles

**NerdWallet (`references/nerdwallet.md`).** NerdWallet is an advice site with an editorial layer, which is the same business as aiabed.ir: articles and guides that earn trust, then a consultation. Its whole system is one deep green with a ladder of pale mints under it, the same structure as the `brand-50` to `brand-900` scale already in `tailwind.config.ts`. Its primary buttons are filled with a deep green and white text. Using our `brand-700` (`#007553`) that way gives a 5.72:1 contrast ratio, which fixes the audit's top problem (white on `#00C389` is 2.29:1). It separates surfaces with mint washes instead of shadows, so it also answers the audit's shadow and gradient findings. Take from it: the mint-wash section panels, the deep-green action and the calm reading pages for the blog. Leave out: its green gradient article band (use a flat field instead) and its hairline-heavy tables outside real tables.

**Wise (`references/wise.md`).** Wise uses a bright green as a fill with near-black text on it, never white. Applied to our colours, `#0F172A` on `#00C389` gives 7.79:1, so the bright brand green can stay as the button colour and still pass. Its other lesson is scale: very heavy display type (weight 900, which Vazirmatn has) set large on a flat, pale field, with no gradients anywhere in the file. That speaks directly to the audit's "no focal point" finding (our largest type is 48px, 3.3vw). Take from it: dark-on-green buttons, a much larger hero headline, flat colour fields. Leave out: its lime colour (we keep `#00C389`) and its soft 24px radius on every card, which the taste skill treats as a weak default.

**Mintlify (`references/mintlify.md`).** Mintlify mixes marketing pages with reading-heavy documentation and uses its mint green sparingly: on the main action, on check icons and as the border of the featured pricing tier. That matches the existing requirement in `CLAUDE.md` that the middle Services card is featured with a green border. Its reading layout is a good model for blog posts, downloads and course pages. Take from it: green as a rare signal rather than a surface, the featured-card treatment and the long-form reading pages. Leave out: its atmospheric gradient heroes, its soft shadows and its dark-mode-first look, which all go against the audit's findings.

**Recommendation: NerdWallet.** It is the closest match in business model (advice plus content, aimed at trust), it reuses the green scale the site already has, and its deep-green button fixes the most serious problem in the audit without changing the brand. Borrow Wise's larger display type for the hero only, when that section is worked on.
